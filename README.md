# MM Generative AI Use Cases

This repository is a working collection of practical **Generative AI**, **AI agent**, and **automation use case ideas**.

The purpose of this repo is to capture day-to-day ideas inspired by current trends in coding, AI, GenAI, cloud, automation, and enterprise technology.

The initial focus is on **warranty-related business use cases**, especially where GenAI can help with claim review, policy interpretation, signal detection, summarization, analyst productivity, and enterprise decision support.

## Purpose

This repository is designed to help evaluate where GenAI may be a good option to address real business needs.

The goal is not to treat GenAI as the answer to every problem. Instead, each use case should clarify:

- The business problem
- The current pain point
- Why GenAI may be useful
- What data would be required
- What human review is needed
- What risks and controls must be considered
- How success could be measured

## Initial Focus Area: Warranty

Warranty is a strong candidate area for GenAI exploration because it includes large volumes of semi-structured and unstructured information, such as:

- Claim narratives
- Repair orders
- Dealer comments
- Technician notes
- Policy documents
- Campaign rules
- Field reports
- Customer concerns
- Diagnostic notes
- Parts replacement history

GenAI can be valuable where teams need to summarize, classify, explain, retrieve, compare, or generate structured outputs from this type of information.

## Types of Use Cases to Capture

Potential categories include:

- Warranty claim review
- Claim explanation generation
- Early warning signal summarization
- Claim-to-policy matching
- Dealer narrative quality checking
- Fraud or anomaly explanation
- Analyst copilots
- Field concern report generation
- Warranty knowledge base Q&A
- Root cause hypothesis support
