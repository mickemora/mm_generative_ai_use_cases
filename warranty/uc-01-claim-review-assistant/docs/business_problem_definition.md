# Business Problem Definition

# UC-01 — Claim Review Assistant

## Executive Summary

The Claim Review Assistant helps analysts perform a comprehensive claim review by orchestrating policy matching, documentation review, narrative review, labor review, campaign review, and repeat repair analysis into a unified workflow.

## Business Opportunity

The solution aims to improve review consistency, reduce manual effort, accelerate claim processing, and improve analyst productivity while preserving human decision authority.

## Design Principle

Decision Support
NOT autonomous claim approval or denial.
