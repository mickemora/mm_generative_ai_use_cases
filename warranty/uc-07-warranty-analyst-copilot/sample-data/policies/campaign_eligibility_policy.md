# Campaign Eligibility Policy

## Purpose

This sample policy defines guidance for evaluating whether a warranty claim may be associated with a service campaign, customer satisfaction program, or recall-related activity.

This document is synthetic and intended only for development, testing, and demonstration of the Warranty Analyst Copilot knowledge base.

## Scope

This policy applies to claims involving:

- Known product concerns
- Active service campaigns
- Customer satisfaction programs
- Recall-related repairs
- VIN-specific eligibility checks

## Eligibility Review Criteria

Analysts should review:

1. Product or VIN eligibility
2. Campaign effective dates
3. Repair completion date
4. Applicable labor operation codes
5. Required repair procedures
6. Required documentation
7. Whether the repair matches campaign scope

## Required Documentation

Claims associated with campaigns should include:

- VIN or product identifier
- Repair order
- Campaign or program code
- Technician confirmation of completed repair
- Supporting diagnostic information when required
- Applicable labor operation

## Analyst Review Guidance

Additional review may be required when:

- The VIN is outside the eligible population
- The repair date falls outside the campaign period
- Required campaign documentation is missing
- The repair performed does not align with campaign instructions
- Incorrect labor operations are used
- Multiple campaigns may apply to the same repair

## Example Missing Information Flags

The following conditions may require follow-up:

- Missing campaign code
- No VIN eligibility confirmation
- Missing repair verification details
- Missing supporting repair documentation
- Labor operation inconsistent with campaign instructions

## Recommended Analyst Action

If campaign eligibility cannot be confirmed, the analyst should request clarification before final claim disposition.

The copilot should identify possible campaign associations, summarize eligibility requirements, and recommend human analyst review.
